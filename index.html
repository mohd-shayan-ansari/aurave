The Auravé Idea

An aura isn't worn.
It's released.

Most fragrance is sold as a finished object — a bottle, a name, a list of notes. We think about it differently. A scent is an event: it opens, it moves, it fades in a particular order, at a particular speed, in a particular direction. Every Auravé blend is built the way an event is directed — top notes cast the opening, heart notes hold the middle, base notes stay behind long after the room has emptied.

Two Crafts, One House

Alcohol and oil.
Speed and patience.

14 Parfums

Eau de Parfum, blended and cut with alcohol for lift and projection. Built for the room you're about to walk into — sprayed, not dabbed, and gone in exactly the order we designed.

10 Attars

Oil-based and alcohol-free, distilled the old deg-bhapka way and aged in sandalwood oil for months, not days. Attar doesn't project across a room — it sits on skin and asks you to lean in.
